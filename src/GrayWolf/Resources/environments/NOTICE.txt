The model preview's environments (image based light)
=====================================================

Each <id>_radiance.dds / <id>_irradiance.dds pair is baked from an HDR panorama by
`GrayWolf.exe bake-environment <panorama.hdr> --id <id> --out <this folder>` (PreviewEnvironment.cpp):
a GGX-prefiltered radiance cube (256 x 256, 6 mips) and an irradiance cube (32 x 32), BC6H, scaled
so their mean light matches the procedural studio sky's. The panoramas are the 2K .hdr files of
Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), published under CC0 1.0 (public domain,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution is required; it is given here.
